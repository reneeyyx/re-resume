An interesting \textbf{origin story} for you: I was 12 when I became addicted to Scratch, a block-based coding platform.
While not a ``real'' language, it taught me how to debug, think algorithmically, and build modular systems: a
foundation that has shaped how I approach every project since.

\vspace{6pt}

Since then, \textbf{I have built and shipped software I genuinely care about.}
At DeltaHacks XII, my team won first place with \underline{\href{https://github.com/dwseoh/quota}{Quota}}, a VS Code extension that cuts API costs by 40\% through
AST-based parsing and a RAG-powered chatbot built with LangChain: a tool I actually use myself.
I also built Nebula, an AI-powered note-taking app using React Native, FastAPI, and semantic search with IVFFlat
indexing, currently in \underline{\href{https://nebula-ai-notes.vercel.app/}{TestFlight}} with CI/CD pipelines and GCP deployment, shipping soon to the App Store.
Beyond personal projects, I volunteered my skills to SolveXchange, a non-profit whose website I rebuilt to
improve accessibility and SEO.

\vspace{6pt}

Professionally, I interned at Pinpoint, an IoT startup for smart spaces, where I taught myself React from scratch,
adopted Git workflows, and independently prototyped and delivered an improved interactive UI in Figma and React.
Most recently, I built full-stack systems on the University of Waterloo Orbital satellite team---developing
React mission control interfaces backed by REST APIs with 95\% test coverage, where correctness
and reliability are non-negotiable.

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{I am drawn to complex problems, especially ones with real impact.}
I scored in the top 7.6\% on the \underline{\href{https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/euclid}{Euclid Math Contest}} and earned Distinction in the \underline{\href{https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/contests/ccc}{CCC}},
and I regularly automate my own workflows with CLI tools and Bash scripts because inefficiency bothers me.

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{I also thrive under pressure and take full ownership.}
As President of \underline{\href{https://hacktheridge.ca/}{Hack The Ridge}} with no prior experience, I organized a 166-person hackathon and raised \$7,920 in
sponsorships. As Sponsorship Director for \underline{\href{https://ignitionhacks.org}{Ignition Hacks}}, I secured \$13,000 in partnerships from companies
including Best Buy and Deloitte. These roles taught me how to communicate clearly under tight deadlines,
manage stakeholders, and deliver: the same skills I bring to every environment.

\vspace{6pt}

Outside of engineering, I am deeply passionate about music. I am a clarinetist, pianist, and composer. But on top of that, I am a conductor, and section leader for large and small ensembles.
Leading ensembles has shaped how I collaborate and lead by example.
